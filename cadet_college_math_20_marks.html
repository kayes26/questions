Full Marks: 20

Question 1 — Multiple Choice    1 × 6 = 6

1. The HCF of two numbers is 12 and their LCM is 720. If one number is 144, what is the other number?
i) 48 ii) 60 iii) 72 iv) 84
2. The five-digit number 47x28 is divisible by 9. What is the value of x?
i) 1 ii) 4 iii) 7 iv) 9
3. Which fraction lies exactly halfway between 3/5 and 5/6?
i) 41/60 ii) 43/60 iii) 17/24 iv) 13/18
4. A number is increased by 20% and then decreased by 20%. If the final number is 384, what was the original number?
i) 384 ii) 400 iii) 420 iv) 480
5. If 2x+1 × 4x−1 = 128, what is the value of x?
i) 1 ii) 2 iii) 3 iv) 4
6. The length of a rectangle is 4 cm more than twice its width. If its perimeter is 44 cm, what is its area?
i) 84 cm² ii) 96 cm² iii) 105 cm² iv) 112 cm²

Question 2 — True / False    1 × 4 = 4

1. If two numbers are co-prime, their LCM is equal to the product of the two numbers.
2. If a proper fraction is multiplied by another proper fraction, the product is smaller than each of the original fractions.
3. A quadrilateral whose diagonals bisect each other is always a parallelogram.
4. If the largest value of a set of data is increased, its median must also increase.

Question 3 — Solve the Problems    10 Marks

1. A student spends 30% of his money on books and then spends 25% of the remaining money on stationery. He has Tk 1,050 left. How much money did he have originally? [3]
2. A hostel has enough food for 600 students for 40 days. After 10 days, 100 students leave. After another 5 days, 200 new students join. For how many more days will the remaining food last? [3]
3. Find the HCF and LCM of 0.72, 1.08 and 1.44. [2]
4. A number is divided by 3 and then 8 is added. The result is equal to half of the original number. Find the number. [2]
