Total Marks: 20 Time: 20 minutes
Answer all questions. Show full working for every question. Marks are indicated in brackets beside each question.
Q1.[3]

Q2.[3]

Q3.[4]
Three pieces of ribbon measure 2.4 m, 3.6 m and 4.2 m. Find the greatest possible length of a single piece of tape that can measure each ribbon exactly, with no length left over.
Q4.[3]
Three traffic signals turn green together at 9:00 a.m. After that, they turn green again at intervals of 0.75 minutes, 1.05 minutes and 1.4 minutes respectively. After how many minutes will all three signals turn green together again?
Q5.[3]

Q6.[4]
