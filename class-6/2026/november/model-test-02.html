Section A — Multiple Choice (10 × 1 = 10 marks)

M1.[1 mark]
Find 3/8 of 96 plus 15% of 200.
(A) 60(B) 64(C) 66(D) 72
M2.[1 mark]
Find the HCF of 54 and 90.
(A) 9(B) 18(C) 27(D) 36
M3.[1 mark]
Evaluate −7 + 4(6 − 2).
(A) −23(B) −9(C) 9(D) 16
M4.[1 mark]
Express 2.4 km in metres.
(A) 240 m(B) 2400 m(C) 24000 m(D) 24 m
M5.[1 mark]
If x = 3, find 5x − 8.
(A) 5(B) 7(C) 9(D) 13
M6.[1 mark]
One angle is 47°. Find its complement.
(A) 43°(B) 47°(C) 53°(D) 133°
M7.[1 mark]
Find the mean of 6, 10, 14 and 18.
(A) 10(B) 11(C) 12(D) 14
M8.[1 mark]
Two quantities are in the ratio 3 : 4 and their sum is 63. Find the larger quantity.
(A) 27(B) 32(C) 35(D) 36
M9.[1 mark]
A price is reduced by 20% and then increased by 20% of the reduced price. Compared with the original, the final price is:
(A) 4% lower(B) unchanged(C) 4% higher(D) 20% lower
M10.[1 mark]
Find the smallest three-digit number divisible by 8, 9 and 12.
(A) 108(B) 120(C) 144(D) 216

Section B — Short Problems (6 × 5 = 30 marks)

M11(a).[2 marks]
Evaluate 7/12 + 5/18. Express your answer in simplest form.
M11(b).[3 marks]
A schoolbag has a marked price of Tk 800. A discount of 15% is offered. Find the selling price.
M12(a).[2 marks]
Find the perimeter of a rectangle whose length is 18 cm and width is 11 cm.
M12(b).[3 marks]
A square garden has side 12 m. Find its area and state the correct unit.
M13.[5 marks]
A water tank is initially 7/8 full. One-third of the water in it is used. Then 15 litres is poured into the tank, making it 3/4 full. Find the capacity of the tank.
M14.[5 marks]
Solve (3x − 5)/4 − (x + 1)/3 = 3. Show the steps clearly.
M15.[5 marks]
A rectangular plot measures 4.5 cm by 3 cm on a plan with scale 1 : 2000. Find the actual length and width in metres, and the actual area in square metres.
M16.[5 marks]
In triangle PQR, side QR is extended beyond R to S. The exterior angle PRS is 130°. The interior angles at P and Q are (2x + 10)° and (3x − 5)°, respectively. Find x and all three interior angles. Give reasons.

Section C — Extended Reasoning (3 × 10 = 30 marks)

M17(a).[3 marks]
A bus is to travel 180 km. It completes 40% of the journey in its first stage. Find the distance travelled in the first stage.
M17(b).[3 marks]
In the second stage, the bus covers 2/3 of the distance remaining after the first stage. Find the second-stage distance.
M17(c).[2 marks]
Find the distance of the final stage.
M17(d).[2 marks]
The average speeds during the first, second and final stages are 48 km/h, 36 km/h and 24 km/h respectively. Find the total driving time (no breaks).
M18(a).[3 marks]
A rectangular garden is 36 m long and 24 m wide. A path 3 m wide runs entirely inside the garden along all four sides. Find the dimensions and area of the inner grassy rectangle.
M18(b).[3 marks]
Find the area covered by the path.
M18(c).[4 marks]
Each paving tile covers 0.36 m². The gardener purchases 8% extra tiles for breakage. Find the total number of tiles to purchase.
M19(a).[2 marks]
Two different positive integers have HCF 12, LCM 720 and sum 228. Find their product using the HCF–LCM identity.
M19(b).[6 marks]
Using the product and sum, determine the two integers. Show why your pair satisfies both conditions.
M19(c).[2 marks]
Check explicitly that the HCF and LCM of your two integers are 12 and 720.
