Class 6 — October 2026 — Cadet College Model Test 02

Subject: MathematicsFull Marks: 70Working: 60 minutesSubmission: 5 minutes
Answer all 17 questions. Show clear working for every question. Any mathematically correct method is accepted. Total time: 65 minutes (60 minutes to solve and 5 minutes to submit).
1.The sum of three consecutive odd numbers is 3 less than four times the smallest number. Find the three numbers. [4 marks]
2.A number leaves remainder 5 when divided by 12, remainder 8 when divided by 15, and remainder 11 when divided by 18. Find the smallest positive such number. [4 marks]
3.Evaluate: (3 1/2 − 5/6) ÷ (7/9 + 1/3) × 4/5. Give the answer as a fraction in simplest form. [4 marks]
4.The greatest common divisor of two positive integers is 18, and their least common multiple is 1,260. If one number is 90, find the other number. [4 marks]
5.The average of 9 numbers is 28. One number, recorded as 46, should have been 19. Find the correct average. [4 marks]
6.A shopkeeper sells 3/8 of a stock of rice in the morning and then 2/5 of the remaining stock in the afternoon. If 45 kg remains, find the original stock. [4 marks]
7.Find the smallest four-digit number that is divisible by each of 24, 36 and 54, but is NOT divisible by 5. [4 marks]
8.Seven numbers in ascending order are 9, 12, 15, x, 21, 24, 28. Their mean is 18. Find x and the median. [4 marks]
9.A bell rings every 18 minutes, a second every 24 minutes, and a third every 30 minutes. All ring together at 8:15 a.m. At what time will they next ring together? [4 marks]
10.Two numbers have sum 168. Their greatest common divisor is 24. If both numbers are greater than 24 and different, find all possible unordered pairs. [4 marks]
11.A vessel is 3/5 full. After 18 litres of water is poured out, it is 7/20 full. What is its capacity? [4 marks]
12.The mean of five distinct positive integers is 16. Their median is 15, their mode does not exist, and their smallest number is 7. Give one possible ordered set of five integers satisfying these conditions. [4 marks]
13.A number between 300 and 500 gives remainder 4 when divided by 7 and remainder 6 when divided by 9. Find all such numbers. [4 marks]
14.A runner completes 5/12 of a route, then 3/7 of what remains. If the distance still left is 10 km, find the length of the entire route. [4 marks]
15.The mean of six scores is 24. When the highest and lowest scores are excluded, the mean of the other four is 25. If the highest score is 40, find the lowest score. [4 marks]
16.A teacher has 144 red pens, 180 blue pens and 252 black pens. She wants to make the greatest possible number of identical packs with no pens left over. How many packs can she make, and how many pens of each colour will be in each pack? [4 marks]
17.Five positive whole numbers have mean 18, median 17 and a unique mode of 14. The smallest number is 10 and the largest is 35. Determine whether such five numbers can exist. Justify your answer. [6 marks]
