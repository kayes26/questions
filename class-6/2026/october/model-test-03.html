Class 6 — October 2026 — Model Test 03

Cadet College Preparation · Mathematics (Reasoning-Focused)

Total: 70 marksSolving: 60 minutesSubmission: 5 minutesMathematics only
Instructions: Answer all 14 questions. Each question carries 5 marks. Show your working clearly, especially where an equation is required. Diagrams are not necessarily drawn to scale. Stop solving after 60 minutes; use the final 5 minutes only to submit your answer script.

Section A — Algebra (45 marks)

Questions M1–M9 · 9 × 5 marks · Suggested 40 minutes

M1.
Simplify 4(2x − 3) − 3[x − 2(4 − x)]. Hence find its value when x = −2. [5 marks]
M2.
Solve the equation 2x − 35 − x + 43 = x − 86. Show your steps. [5 marks]
M3.
Factorise 18p − 30q + 42 by taking out the greatest possible positive integer common factor. Hence, if 18p − 30q + 42 = 90, find 3p − 5q + 7. [5 marks]
M4.
A two-digit number has digits whose sum is 11. Reversing its digits produces a number 27 less than the original. Find the original number. Use equations and verify your answer. [5 marks]
M5.
A number sequence increases by the same amount each step. Its first four terms are 7, 12, 17 and 22. Find a formula for the nth term (n = 1 for the first term). Is 132 a term of the sequence? If so, state its position. [5 marks]
M6.
A purse contains only Tk 5 and Tk 10 coins. The number of Tk 5 coins is 3 more than twice the number of Tk 10 coins. The total value is Tk 255. Using an equation, find how many coins of each type are in the purse. [5 marks]
M7.
Ten years ago, a mother was seven times as old as her daughter. Ten years from now, the mother will be twice as old as her daughter. Use equations to find their present ages. Check both statements. [5 marks]
M8.
If 3a − 2b = 9, find both 12a − 8b and 5(3a − 2b) − (6a − 4b) + 7. You do not need to find a and b separately. [5 marks]
M9.
Two expressions, 5(2x − 3) − 4(x + 2) and 3(x + 7) − (x + 4), have equal values. Form an equation, find x and then calculate their common value. [5 marks]

Section B — Other Mathematics (25 marks)

Questions M10–M14 · 5 × 5 marks · Suggested 20 minutes

M10.
A shopkeeper marks a bag 25% above its cost price and then gives a 12% discount on the marked price. The shopkeeper still makes a profit of Tk 110. Find the cost price and the marked price. [5 marks]
M11.
On a scale plan of a rectangular field, its length is 3.2 cm and its width is 1.8 cm. The scale is 1:5000. Calculate its actual area in hectares. (1 hectare = 10,000 m².) [5 marks]
M12.
In triangle ABC, BC is extended beyond C to D. The exterior angle ACD is 130°. The ratio of ∠BAC to ∠ABC is 2 : 3. Find all three interior angles of triangle ABC. The sketch is not to scale. [5 marks]
M13.
Originally, the numbers of boys and girls in a club were in the ratio 3 : 5. After 8 more boys joined, the ratio became 5 : 7. Find the original numbers of boys and girls. [5 marks]
M14.
A boat covers 24 km downstream in 2 hours and travels back along the same route in 3 hours. Assuming that both the boat’s speed in still water and the river current are constant, find both speeds in km/h. [5 marks]
