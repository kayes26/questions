Section C — Unitary Method & Food Supply (10 marks)

Answer both parts; show all calculations.
M3(a) [4 marks]
A boarding school has enough rice to feed 1,200 students for 40 days. After 10 days, 300 additional students arrive. For how many more days will the remaining rice last if everyone eats the same amount each day?
M3(b) [6 marks]
After those 300 students arrive, the school runs for another 12 days. Then 300 students leave. How many additional days will the remaining rice last?

Section D — Mensuration (10 marks)

Give proper units in your answers.
M4(a) [5 marks]
A rectangular garden is 12 m longer than it is wide. A uniform 3 m wide path is built all around the garden, outside its boundary. If the perimeter of the outer rectangle is 228 m, find the length and width of the garden.
M4(b) [5 marks]
Two straight paths, each 3 m wide, cross at right angles through the entire garden: one parallel to its length and one parallel to its width. Find the total area occupied by these two paths inside the garden.

Section E — Percentage & Work (10 marks)

Show complete reasoning and calculations.
M5(a) [4 marks]
A shop sells a schoolbag for Tk 1,610 after allowing a discount of 12.5% on its marked price. Find the marked price.
M5(b) [6 marks]
A contractor planned to complete a job in 24 days using 180 workers, all working at the same rate. After 8 days, the contractor is told to finish the remaining work in 12 more days. How many extra workers must be hired immediately?

Section F — Algebra & Mathematical Reasoning (10 marks)

M6(b) [5 marks]
